# Email Templates for Lead Nurturing

## 1. Warm Lead - Initial Contact

**Subject:** Want to explore our product?

**Body:**
```
Hi {{name}},

We noticed you logged into the platform recently.

Would you like to schedule a demo?

Best regards,
Your Team
```

---

## 2. Warm Lead - Follow-up (Day 2)

**Subject:** Case study: How companies use our platform

**Body:**
```
Hi {{name}},

Here's a case study showing how companies like yours use our platform to achieve results.

[Link to case study]

Would you like to discuss how this could work for you?

Best regards,
Your Team
```

---

## 3. Cold Lead - Re-engagement

**Subject:** We miss you!

**Body:**
```
Hi {{name}},

We noticed you haven't logged in recently.

Is there anything we can help you with?

Best regards,
Your Team
```

---

## 4. Hot Lead - Immediate Contact

**Subject:** Let's schedule a call

**Body:**
```
Hi {{name}},

We see you're actively using the platform!

Would you like to schedule a call to discuss how we can help you get even more value?

Best regards,
Your Team
```
